There’s no single official “biggest club in the world,” because people measure it differently — by trophies, fanbase, revenue, history, or global influence. But the clubs most commonly mentioned are:

* Real Madrid — Often considered the biggest overall because of its global fanbase, record UEFA Champions League titles, and historic success.
* Manchester United — One of the largest fanbases worldwide and huge commercial influence.
* FC Barcelona — Massive global support and one of football’s most iconic clubs.
* Liverpool FC — Historic European success and worldwide supporters.
* Bayern Munich — Dominant in Germany with a strong global reputation.

If you go by:

* **Most Champions League titles:** Real Madrid
* **Highest revenue/value regularly:** Real Madrid or Manchester United
* **Largest fanbases globally:** usually Real Madrid, FC Barcelona, and Manchester United

Most football fans and analysts today would probably answer: **Real Madrid**.
